Trading journal

See what your trading habits cost you.

Edgeline logs every trade, measures it against the plan you had going in, and scores how you traded: sizing, overtrading, what you do after a loss. Then it names the one habit to fix next.

01

Every trade, in R

The journal is the home page: what is open and how much is at risk, what closed and what it made.

  • Results in R and in money, net of fees.
  • Bring trades in from Binance or Hyperliquid history, a CSV, or a pasted screenshot.
  • A daily guard that warns at your loss limit and after a losing streak.
Open the journal full size
02

One score, one thing to fix

The Edge score sits in the header on every page. It grades how you traded, not what you made.

  • Eight habits, each 0 to 100: sizing, overtrading, after a loss, plan, loss control, exits, mistakes, review.
  • The habit taking the most off the score is named, with the trades behind it.
  • A concrete step to raise it, and whether you are up or down on last month.
Open the score full size
03

Each trade against its plan

Open any trade to see it on the chart with the entry, stop and target you set.

  • How far it ran for you and against you while you were in.
  • What the plan would have made if you had left it alone.
  • Grades for the entry, the stop and the exit, plus your notes and screenshots.
Open a trade full size
04

Where the edge comes from

Stats splits the record every way that matters, so a good month cannot hide a bad habit.

  • Drawdown, streaks and the shape of your results.
  • What your management adds or costs against leaving trades alone.
  • Mistakes ranked by what they cost, limit against market entries, size against result.

05

The month at a glance

The calendar shows each day's result and trade count, with a note for the day.

  • Green and red days in money, R or win rate.
  • Click a day for its trades and what you wrote.
Open the calendar full size
06

A review you will actually do

The week's trades in a row, one decision each: traded well, traded poorly, or tilt.

  • A counter of what is still waiting, so nothing is skipped.
  • Check the week against the venue's own history from a screenshot.
Open the review full size
07

What is strong right now

The screener ranks markets by strength against BTC and flags the ones accelerating.

  • Crypto, plus the equities, indices and commodities listed on Hyperliquid.
  • Flags for candidates, divergences and leverage-driven moves.
  • A track record of whether past flags went on to beat BTC.

In the demo the screener is a view of the latest scan; it cannot be refreshed.
